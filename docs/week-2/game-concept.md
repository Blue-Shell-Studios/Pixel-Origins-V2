# Pixel Origins
*Step outside your role and become the hero the world wasn't ready for.*

## Core Gameplay Loop
- Explore the world.
- Defeat enemies.
- Solve puzzles.
- Take quests.
- Overcome adversities.

## Genre and Inspirations
- Primary genre: Top-down adventure, hack and slash, and puzzle-solving.

### Reference Games
- **Evoland 1 and 2**: Overall game inspiration.
- **Oceanhorn, Darkrise, and Swordigo**: Player arsenal and combat mechanics.
- **Knights of Pen and Paper**: Quest structure and pacing.

## Technical Feasibility
### Required Systems
- Movement and combat
- Inventory and item use
- Enemy AI
- Environmental hazards
- Puzzle system
- Dialogue and quest system
- UI/HUD

### Primary Technical Risk
The biggest technical risk are event management and puzzle map design.

### Validation Plan (Week 5)
Feasibility will be validated by implementing:
- A controllable player character
- A simple enemy
- A working sword combat mechanic

## Scope Management
### Minimum Viable Product (Core Loop Only)
- Playable character with sword, shield, and basic combat mechanics
- Vision Visor ability plus one puzzle-focused ability (Bow or Fire Spark)
- Fully playable Starting Town and Overgrown Forest
- 3-5 main quests with 1-2 optional side quests
- Basic puzzles and exploration

### Stretch Goals (If Time Permits)
- Unlock City and Ruins and integrate the full player arsenal
- Add all planned biomes with fully integrated environmental hazards
- Add optional multi-step puzzles

### Explicitly Out of Scope
- Branching questlines and multiple endings
- Additional biomes and excessive items or abilities
- Advanced enemy AI, leveling, or stat systems
